Q
Does the iPhone 15 Pro Max support dual SIM with 5G on both?
Yes, the iPhone 15 Pro Max supports Dual SIM (nano-SIM + eSIM) and both can use 5G simultaneously in India. You can have two different numbers active at the same time. The device supports up to 8 eSIMs and dual eSIM setup.
Answered by Apple India Official Store · 248 people found this helpful
Q
Does it come with a charger in the box?
No, the iPhone 15 Pro Max comes only with a USB-C to USB-C cable in the box. No power adapter is included. Apple sells adapters separately. You'll need at least a 20W USB-C adapter for fast charging, or a 30W+ adapter for the fastest MagSafe charging.
Answered by 142 customers · 312 people found this helpful
Q
Is the Indian variant different from the US model?
The Indian (A3293) and US (A3105) models are almost identical. Both support mmWave 5G now in India. The main difference is that the Indian variant has a physical SIM + eSIM slot, while the US model is eSIM-only. Both have identical cameras and performance.
Answered by Karan Verma (Top Reviewer) · 198 people found this helpful
Q
Can I play Genshin Impact and Call of Duty at max settings?
Absolutely. The A17 Pro chip handles both games at maximum graphics with hardware-accelerated ray tracing. Genshin at 60fps maximum settings with ray tracing enabled is silky smooth. CoD Mobile at 120fps max settings runs without any drops. The only thing to note is the phone gets warm after extended gaming (45+ mins).
Answered by 87 customers · 156 people found this helpful
